# Research protocol: self-managed household Stromwechsel

Apply this shared protocol to the mode and inputs established in SKILL.md. Use the mode-specific completion criteria in the output section; no full comparison is implied by an initial watchlist.

## 1. Scenario and defaults

Keep the **actual intended Lieferbeginn** distinct from any **current quotation Lieferbeginn** used for simulation or an illustrative preliminary price snapshot. Explain the chosen quotation date and its evidence: **model-only**, **selected in the calculator**, or **compatibility established by applicable terms**. These can coexist; selecting a date is not supplier acceptance, and a model-only date is not verified availability. If the calculator has no date field, check applicable timing conditions without submitting an application; do not demand a binding confirmation for a simulation. Apply all tariff checks, exclusions and comparisons to that one quotation scenario, address and primary Jahresverbrauch; do not reject a simulated offer because the actual date is outside its booking window. Separately record that real-world timing limit. A price for another location or consumption basis is not an address-specific quote.

Unless overridden, briefly declare these defaults without requiring a questionnaire:

- Festpreistarif; initial term at most 12 months (shorter terms remain eligible).
- Preisgarantie lasting through the initial term; check its scope, not just its duration.
- Kündigungsfrist at most one month, monthly Abschläge, no Vorkasse, Kaution or Pakettarif.
- Preference for 100% renewables plus independently evidenced additional Energiewende contribution.
- For intentional annual switchers, rank primarily by effective first-year cost with attainable bonuses; show base/no-bonus cost separately. For longer stays, give ongoing/multi-year economics more weight.

Treat contract/payment defaults as hard filters, and stronger Ökostrom as a preference unless the user makes it mandatory. Do not relax filters silently for a cheap offer. A guarantee can exclude components without fixing the whole bill; disclose these exclusions. Do not assume that a recommended guarantee is legally required or worth any premium.

## 2. Discovery and sources

### Consumer guidance

At the start of substantive research check current accessible guidance from **Verbraucherzentrale**, **Stiftung Warentest** and **Finanztip** on tariff selection, contracts, provider risks and comparison portals. Cite retrieval/publication dates and explain material differences or adaptations for the user's strategy, including attainable bonuses for annual switching. These organizations' advice is current evidence, not immutable skill defaults. Use accessible material when a paywall or technical restriction blocks a source, name the gap and never invent the unavailable content. Consumer guidance is distinct from using a portal or Finanztip comparison to discover offers.

### Bounded discovery, focused verification

Allocate effort by decision value. Discovery and screening counts are planning guides, not quotas; the Switching Simulation verification minimum below is a completion requirement:

1. **Discovery:** make a quick market scan of up to roughly 20–30 unique potentially suitable tariff offers, including direct and portal channels; this is not a full contract/document review of each offer. A portal's result count is not the number inspected; the same offer repeated is not new coverage.
2. **Screening:** remove duplicates and screen for mandatory conditions, price and known eligibility/timing constraints. Keep unverified conditions distinct from confirmed exclusions.
3. **Verification:** investigate roughly 3–5 promising offers, prioritizing enough to establish at least three fully verified eligible offers from different providers in Switching Simulation. Close material questions using the checks below: supply-point pricing, contract, each bonus, mandatory extras, Abschläge, environmental quality and comparative operational risks. Prioritize these gaps over collecting more names.
4. **Conditional expansion:** if verification leaves too few viable alternatives for a reasoned choice or fewer than three verified eligible offers in Switching Simulation, return to discovery for another batch, roughly up to 20 additional offers as useful. Do not expand merely to inflate the initial discovery count.

**Switching Simulation verification gate:** Complete evidence-based verification of **at least three eligible tariff offers from three different providers**, before presenting a completed simulated choice. For each, verify the actual supply-point price, mandatory contract and payment terms, scope/duration of Preisgarantie, each materially relevant bonus and its applicable eligibility/payout conditions, metering/extra charges, and material environmental/operational risks. Locate, inspect and retain the applicable available offer documents (such as Preisblatt or offer summary, AGB, Bonusbedingungen and product-specific conditions); separate PDFs are not required when authoritative applicable terms are published in accessible HTML, but capture their source and version. A downloaded general AGB, a portal card, a price with unassessed bonus conditions, or an excluded tariff does **not** count as a verified eligible offer. If fewer than three qualify, continue targeted verification and expand candidates rather than treating recorded gaps as an acceptable stopping point. The user may explicitly authorize a smaller comparison; do not silently reduce the minimum.

After Screening, use **Question-driven Verification**: keep a small, evolving set of unresolved questions in the existing report, not a separate tracker. Prioritize **decision-critical** questions affecting mandatory suitability or a defensible choice, **decision-relevant** questions affecting effective cost, bonus attainability or material operational/financial risks, and **informational** details. A bonus that could change the ranking deserves early attention. Before a substantial check, know the question, how its answer could change the decision, and which accessible source can provide suitable evidence with the least reasonable effort. These priorities guide work; do not document a category before every browser action or duplicate the checks below.

Resolve the most valuable questions before filling fields or enlarging the archive. A minor contradiction need not block selection when all established alternatives meet mandatory requirements and its remaining uncertainty cannot materially change the choice; disclose it. Stop when verified evidence supports a reasoned choice with honest remaining uncertainties, rather than absolute document completeness.

For each material question distinguish **verified**, **attempted but blocked** (name the concrete obstacle and alternative sources tried), and **not yet checked**. An unfinished check is not an external blocker or permission to finish. Before abandoning a decision-critical question after reasonable public-source attempts, initiate a concrete human handoff when assistance could supply the missing evidence; name the exact offer, condition, source and user action. Do not silently substitute a saved incomplete report for a usable handoff. Scale depth to the mode: Preliminary Research may stop at an explicitly preliminary watchlist; Switching Simulation must meet its completion criteria below. No fixed limit on sites or attempts is implied.

Search provider-direct offers and calculators as well as available portal leads; include relevant local/regional providers and offers outside the initial seed set. Portals, popularity, sponsored positions, badges and review averages are discovery signals, not ranking authorities. When direct and portal offers differ, check their prices and terms separately.

Use sources according to the claim:

- Offer-specific facts: official calculator, Preisblatt, Vertragszusammenfassung, AGB and bonus terms; resolve which version/campaign applies.
- Consumer/regulatory context: Verbraucherzentralen/vzbv, Stiftung Warentest, Bundesnetzagentur, Schlichtungsstelle Energie and relevant court/regulator material.
- Independent context: well-supported specialist reporting such as Finanztip.
- Operational experience: dated reviews, forums and community reports; distinguish allegations from documented findings.

For a specific offer use **Public Offer Details → Applicable Documents → Safe Public Alternatives → Human Handoff**. Before downloading general AGB or advancing through portal steps, inspect relevant public tabs and disclosures of the current offer. Discover the current interface rather than assuming button names or identical layouts across viewport sizes, sessions or offer versions. For material missing terms, actively check the current offer's expanded sections and downloads, the provider's official product pages and documents, and applicable documents exposed through portals such as Verivox or CHECK24; a portal-linked PDF is usable only after verifying the exact provider, tariff, channel, campaign/version and applicability. Prefer the supplier's applicable original documents where obtainable, and resolve channel/version differences instead of assuming equivalence. Documents remain necessary where they control a material question, such as bonus eligibility, guarantee exclusions, metering or certification. Before reading one, identify the question it can answer; if it lacks the needed information, change sources instead of repeatedly extracting general text. Merely noting a missing Bonusbedingungen or AGB is not an adequate verification attempt.

Cite sources near material claims and preserve the evidence's association with product, channel, available offer version/date, calculation inputs and the specific condition; record retrieval dates. Search snippets and generic marketing cannot settle missing offer-specific conditions. Save or identify controlling offer documents for the user's verification before they act. Where feasible, retain compact evidence of otherwise changing interactive quotes (a result excerpt, table or screenshot) with calculation inputs, channel and date. Minimize personal data and refer to the private profile instead of repeating the full address; no tracing, video or new dependency is required. Do not invent missing rates, bonuses, availability or meter requirements.

### Comparison portal discovery

Use available CHECK24 or Verivox results alongside direct offers, adapting their settings to the current consumer guidance above. Finanztip can provide supplementary discovery or independent context; no integration is required. One portal plus official sites can suffice when they provide enough competitive variety for a reasoned choice; use another portal when it could materially improve coverage or resolve an important uncertainty. Neither both portals nor a user-produced export is mandatory. A supplied export, screenshot or copied results can broaden discovery. Inspect current filters and sorting before operating or advising on the interface; do not prescribe remembered buttons.

Set the agreed location/consumption and mandatory term, guarantee, notice, payment and environmental requirements where supported; check unsupported conditions independently. Explicitly sort ordinary results by the relevant annual cost rather than popularity or advertising order, and separate sponsored placements even when they ignore filters. For annual switching, discover by first-year cost including advertised bonuses, then verify attainability and also examine competitive offers without bonus dependence. Remove unjustified direct-signup-only, high-recommendation, portal-recommended or regional-only restrictions. Showing one tariff per provider can economize initial discovery; inspect alternative products of promising providers during verification. Independently verify portal prices, each bonus and material terms; portal ranking does not establish suitability.

Exports can omit checkbox/radio state. Treat the user's explicit filter statement as authoritative; absent state is unknown, not disabled. Flag material visible conflicts without restarting the search merely because an export cannot prove the settings.

### Price access limitations

Establish that each price applies to the actual supply point and Netzgebiet. Enter the complete supply location when needed, including a separate Zusatz when exposed. A postcode-only calculator can suffice if official evidence establishes applicability to the relevant network; entering a street alone does not prove it. Record the location/network basis and unresolved zone ambiguity. If a calculator additionally needs a MaLo-ID, meter information or an unavailable interactive step, identify the requirement without fabricating a price or entering an application. Use a reasonable alternative source or calculator where practical; no quota of sites is required.

For missing prices distinguish **provider restriction**, **tool limitation** and **deliberately limited research scope**. A distant date is not itself proof that today's prices cannot be researched. Switching Simulation requires actual attempts to obtain current address-specific prices; a provider's booking window can justify a different quotation date, not abandonment of pricing. If a material check remains blocked and human help could safely resolve it, offer assistance before treating that route as exhausted; follow [documents and user actions](documents-and-actions.md) for domain limits, retained evidence and unresolved user tasks. If access remains blocked, report an incomplete simulation and the concrete next step rather than substituting unrelated example prices.

## 3. Contract checks and economics

For each serious offer establish:

- Arbeitspreis in ct/kWh, monthly/annual Grundpreis, gross/net basis and separately charged metering costs (including mME/iMSys where applicable).
- Initial term, Kündigungsfrist, continuation and payment cadence/Abschlag logic; exclude advance-payment/deposit/package tariffs under the defaults.
- Guarantee duration and covered components; explicitly identify exclusions for Steuern, Abgaben, Umlagen, Netzentgelte and Messstellenbetrieb. Check whether it begins at contract conclusion or supply start and lasts through the chosen initial term.
- Sofortbonus, Neukundenbonus and other credits, each with its own evidence and classification below.

Calculate transparently using the primary annual consumption:

`annual base cost = kWh × Arbeitspreis / 100 + annual Grundpreis + mandatory extras not already included`

`first-year effective cost = annual base cost - attainable bonuses/credits`

Avoid double-counting metering charges or confusing an instalment with effective monthly cost. Distinguish supplier charges from portal metadata that merely lacks information; resolve material conflicts against applicable terms. Assess payment count, Abschlag amount, Verbrauchsprognose, adjustment options and Guthaben timing separately from tariff cost. Excessive instalments can burden liquidity even if later refunded; do not treat the temporary overpayment itself as an extra annual energy charge. Identify intermediary fees if applicable and documented; do not infer an ongoing service agreement or expand into exit-term research without the opt-in required by SKILL.md.

Use A bonuses in the primary effective-cost calculation. Show B-dependent cost separately as conditional, alongside the no-bonus downside; exclude C or unassessed amounts. A large bonus is not inherently bad. The question is attainability under ordinary termination at the intended term end, not its size.

Compare alternatives against the current contract using a consistent time basis. Do not reuse a current first-year bonus as a renewal benefit. Base cost is an unchanged-rate second-year indicator, not a guarantee of future prices. Explain shorter-term or calendar-based products on their actual terms instead of assuming every contract lasts exactly a year.

## 4. Bonus classification

Classify **each identified bonus under the applicable offer terms**, not a supplier's reputation or generic advertising. Essential checks are:

- Neukunde definition and same-supplier/corporate-group look-back, matched against the user's history.
- Minimum actual Belieferungsdauer versus Vertragslaufzeit; delayed start, moving or early termination rules where relevant.
- Compatibility with timely ordinary Kündigung effective at the intended end date; `ungekündigt` conditions or requirements extending beyond the chosen term.
- Amount/basis, who pays the bonus, payout date/channel, clawback and any restrictions on redeeming or paying out credits.

| Class / evidence state                       | Meaning and economic treatment                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A - confirmed compatible**                 | Applicable material terms and eligibility are verified and compatible with the scenario, with no material conflict unresolved. Use in primary economics. This is contractual compatibility, not proof of future payout.                   |
| **B - compatible with a bounded caveat**     | Material eligibility and termination conditions are checked; a specific remaining caveat is identified and does not hide an unknown essential condition. Show conditional economics separately and explain what must be resolved.         |
| **C - evidenced risk or incompatibility**    | A specific applicable condition or documented conflict makes receipt unsafe/incompatible. Explain the evidence and exclude from primary economics.                                                                                        |
| **Not yet assessed - insufficient evidence** | Essential terms, applicable campaign/version or eligibility are missing/conflicting. Do not assign A/B, call it a proven negative risk, or count the amount. State what is missing. This is an evidence state, not a fourth safety class. |

For each unassessed bonus name its own missing material condition. Keep bonus eligibility, unknown cost components and overall offer suitability separate: an unknown metering charge alone does not make a bonus unattainable, and an assessed bonus does not establish the full price.

Use the same standard across candidates. Record operational evidence about disputed bonus payout separately; a contractual A does not override an evidenced payout problem or imply that complaint research was completed. An invoice credit or promised bonus is not confirmation of bank settlement.

## 5. Environmental quality and operational reliability

Distinguish 100% renewable electricity backed by Herkunftsnachweise from independently evidenced additional Energiewende contribution. Check the exact tariff's current Grüner Strom Label, ok-power, newer-plant criteria, renewable investment or equivalent evidence. A green brand or portal badge is insufficient; certification need not cover every product. Explain ecological trade-offs without turning a preference into an unrequested hard exclusion.

For operational reliability investigate recurring, recent evidence around Kündigung/extension, Zählerstand and Schätzung, annual/final billing, Guthaben, bonus payment, excessive Abschläge, correction delays, support and failed switches. Separate individual allegations, repeated patterns and documented findings; do not infer a pattern from one review or reliability from a star average. Keep product-specific problems distinct (for example dynamic billing versus fixed supply, or membership versus supply contracts).

After screening, substantively compare finalists on the decision-relevant operational risks above, prioritizing observed issues and the user's experience. Explain how evidence, recency, resolution and remaining gaps affect the recommendation. A preliminary scan for signals is not a completed comparative reliability assessment. State scope and gaps consistently, without requiring equal review counts or treating no found complaint as proof of reliability.

### Personal provider experience

Use **Personal Experience → Targeted Investigation → Risk Assessment → Recommendation**. Separate the event and its evidence (user report, document or verified outcome), the user's attitude/preferences, and independent findings about current conditions or recurrence. Consider positive as well as negative experiences and their age/product relevance. Investigate the corresponding risk when the user expresses distrust; neither ignore it nor automatically exclude the provider. A personal incident can matter to the user's choice without proving a company-wide pattern. Honor explicit exclusions as preferences, not general factual findings.

**Hypothetical example:** expected energy cost is about EUR 90/month, but a provider demands EUR 190–200 in Abschläge and later returns a large Guthaben. A EUR 40 annual tariff saving may not justify the cash-flow burden for that user. Check the consumption forecast, ability and effort to reduce instalments, and risk of delayed refunds; explain these separately from annual price and let the user's liquidity preference affect the recommendation. Do not record this example as an actual user event. Store preferences and dated experiences using the private workspace locations in SKILL.md; never put real personal financial history in public skill instructions.

For frequent switching, check current acceptance evidence and relevant corporate relationships. Distinguish old Bonusjäger reports from current policy; the user's own rejections are particularly relevant. Low consumption alone is not evidence of rejection risk. Where acceptance uncertainty matters, keep viable application alternatives for the user rather than drifting into a poor fallback after failures.

## 6. Strategic reserves

A reserve is a provider-level fallback for a reasonable stay of about 2-3 years, not a temporarily cheap bonus tariff or a requirement to sign a 2-3 year contract.

- **Reserve candidate:** being evaluated; remains eligible for ordinary annual optimization.
- **Designated Strategic Reserve:** explicitly agreed and appointed by the user; exclude from ordinary optimization and label **do not use in normal switching** until the user changes that decision.

Propose a small set, typically 1-2, only when evidence supports suitability. Evaluate location-specific ordinary pricing, manageable terms, operational reliability, likelihood of supply and usefulness for a longer stay. Consider local/nationwide diversification and past use without automatically disqualifying a previous provider. Do not select by the largest bonus. If price, service or suitability remains unverified, keep the proposal unconfirmed or state that no suitable reserve is established; do not fill a quota.

Repeated rejections or clear switching friction may justify moving from optimize to stabilize and recommending reserve activation to the user. Roughly 2-3 failed applications can prompt reassessment, not an automatic rule: consider reasons, remaining time and alternatives. Do not silently redesignate or activate reserves.

## 7. Report and completion criteria

Use the workspace persistence and language rules in SKILL.md. Structure a compact decision report around the following information; prefer a readable comparison table plus short notes over paragraphs inside wide cells.

- **Context:** at the start, summarize important unresolved user actions if any, with their effect and links to the yearly register; keep the report self-contained. Include researched-at date, mode, actual intended and quotation Lieferbeginn, primary kWh and its provenance, location reference to the private profile, current benchmark, strategy, defaults/overrides and missing inputs.
- **Options:** a small watchlist/shortlist, normally no more than 3-5 ordinary candidates unless requested. Lead with verified base costs and suitability, then show first-year cost using the bonus treatment above. Keep unassessed advertising potential separate from the decision ranking. For serious offers show provider/tariff/channel, rates, mandatory extras, each bonus and evidence state, instalment/payment count, term/notice/continuation, guarantee scope, Ökostrom evidence, comparative operational assessment and acceptance caveats, source and offer dates. Keep unknown fields unknown, never zero; identify the material check states established during verification.
- **Reserves:** separate candidates from designated providers, explain evidence and gaps, preserve existing decisions.
- **Near-misses:** include only useful exclusions grounded in the selected quotation scenario; distinguish failure of a filter from an unverified condition or a real-date booking limitation.
- **Decision:** explain trade-offs and the next step for the user, including the exact offer terms/documents still needed before their own application or cancellation.

### Preliminary Research completion

Established context, investigated market options, material conditions/risks and explicit evidence gaps are sufficient for a preliminary watchlist. Explain which current prices were obtained, which were not and why, and whether an earlier quotation date was used. Do not label the watchlist a verified price ranking or claim the market prevented pricing when pricing was simply outside the chosen scope.

### Switching Simulation completion

Compare at least three evidence-verified eligible current address-specific offers from different providers for the chosen quotation scenario and show base and first-year economics with the bonus treatment above, unless the user explicitly authorizes a narrower comparison. Explicitly separate the simulated choice from feasibility at the actual intended start. Apply the decision-value standard above: disclosed nonmaterial uncertainties do not prevent completion. If the minimum remains unmet after focused verification, expanded discovery and reasonable alternative sources, request a specific human handoff for material evidence the user could provide. Save partial results, the attempts and blockers, and label the simulation incomplete while awaiting assistance or if no permissible route remains; do not simply list unchecked questions and conclude, or silently downgrade it to completed Preliminary Research.

Before delivering, check that the conclusion matches the evidence and mode, all comparisons use the same scenario and consumption, reserve decisions remain intact, new household documents follow the chosen working language, and the dated report/annual overview are actually saved with previous reports preserved. Report persistence failure honestly.
