# Electricity Switching

The skill follows the **Progressive Discovery Funnel**, performs research directly, and saves progress during lengthy work and resumable results at phase boundaries. Read and follow the instructions for the current implemented phase; do not use separate Research or Execution modes.

After identifying the workspace, read and follow the [shared task instructions](references/shared/tasks/instructions.md) whenever it contains active tasks or a phase reviews documents for a concrete provider contract, identifies a provider-to-user payment or credit, or identifies another follow-up. Apply the same procedure to user-initiated task updates after a checkpoint in this conversation, even when the skill is not named again. Within the current phase's task-selection scope, use the shared eligibility and receipt follow-up procedure for provider-to-user payments or credits even when either status is unknown. Apply the phase's scope filter before creating tasks from historical records. This rule does not apply to general market-search candidates.

When reviewing the user's actual supply contracts or billing records, read and follow the [shared history instructions](references/shared/history/instructions.md) to maintain the document-based supply history in the user's workspace.

Across phases, use the [shared model escalation instructions](references/shared/model-escalation/instructions.md) when a material unresolved question could change a conclusion or the user or a phase explicitly requires a stronger-model review. Apply the provider-specific request procedure only when the runtime supports it.

For lengthy reviews and interrupted work, follow the [working checkpoint instructions](references/shared/checkpoints/instructions.md). Keep progress files separate from concise durable state.
