# Final acceptance and completion

Enter only when all required planned work has been addressed and accepted batch checkpoints are present. Account for every suspended or superseded batch and remaining obligation; no unresolved required work or pending required batch gate. Inspect actual evidence and dependencies, not just plan status. Use [Reviewers](reviewers.md), [Review protocol](review-protocol.md) and [Evidence and checkpoints](evidence-and-checkpoints.md).

## 1. Final Substantive

Start a fresh isolated Final Substantive Reviewer. Initial handoff prioritizes original request, human-approved Brief/Plan, effective accepted amendments and human decisions, governing methodology/sources, actual implementation/results and reproducible primary evidence. Do not limit review by Executor summaries, last batch or listed E-Steps. Check whole Task correctness, assumptions, interactions, scientific/technical/domain validity, limitations and reproducibility. Negative/inconclusive findings can satisfy obligations if method and evidence are valid.

Require explicit full-task substantive acceptance on identifiable current governing definition, sources, relevant implementation/evidence. For blocking findings or elected corrections requiring new work, run normal new correction batches and dependency revalidation under [Changes and corrections](changes-and-corrections.md). After checkpointing corrections, re-establish full-task substantive acceptance on new state. Same final reviewer may do targeted follow-up only when it independently verifies affected scope/downstream impact and explicitly re-establishes whole-task acceptance. Broad/interacting changes, uncertainty or invalid context require fresh full substantive review.

## 2. Final Completeness

Only after substantive acceptance, reconcile current-stage pointers and summaries where possible (see Close). If that changes substantive content, re-establish substantive acceptance first; then start a different fresh isolated Final Completeness Reviewer. Give original request, full authoritative Brief/Plan, amendments/decisions, Execution Plan, Task Ledger, actual artifacts/evidence, and substantive acceptance. Check every mandatory Objective and obligation, actual execution (including evidence of applicable accepted strategic Tools & Skills use, required workflows and authorized material deviations across the effective definition), Expected Evidence/Verification, tracking accuracy across the Task lifecycle (including required Ledger events, identities, compliance with the applicable [Task Ledger timestamp policy](task-ledger.md#timestamp-capture), authoritative pointers and reconciled historical adoption where applicable), omitted work and blockers. Review the identifiable submitted history; subsequent actual review/closure events are recorded and verified at Close against actual responses, checkpoint, governing decisions and the same timestamp policy and material progress/waiting discipline, not required as fictional future evidence. Missing E-Step is not an excuse. Assess material compliance with the [Progress invariant and Waiting discipline](../SKILL.md#pending-work-and-waiting) within the applicable governing revision and submitted scope, using available evidence. Evaluate the purpose of operations and whether the enabled next action was taken; identify demonstrated unjustified activity, waiting substitution or unsupported cessation, not merely particular commands. Apply the review protocol's disposition of historical process deviations; do not require exhaustive raw-session replay, additional telemetry or a separate gate. Require explicit acceptance of entire obligation set.

For blocking findings or elected corrections that require new substantive work or revalidation, Executor plans and executes new correction batches (reviewer proposes, never edits), checks dependencies and checkpoints. Re-establish substantive acceptance **before** concluding completeness on changed relevant content. Then re-review completeness; narrow follow-up may retain reviewer only with explicit entire-obligation acceptance of new state, broad changes or invalid context require fresh review. Never overlap active reviewers. For a historical process finding requiring only record reconciliation or independent disposition, correct the remediable effects and resubmit to the applicable existing review without inventing executable work. Low findings alone do not require correction; record disposition. Pure lifecycle metadata does not alter substantive content.

## 3. Close

As part of existing closeout (not another gate), reconcile current-stage pointers in Plan/Task Ledger/Context index, label historical observations as of their actual stage, and remove stale prospective wording from current summaries. Do not rewrite historical evidence. Prefer this hygiene before completeness submission. After either acceptance, verify which reviewed claims changed: recording a gate or correcting purely presentational metadata need not trigger review, but changes to actual tracking accuracy, obligation dispositions or evidence require renewed affected acceptance. If substantive state changed, re-establish substantive acceptance before completeness. If only completeness-relevant records changed, obtain explicit whole-obligation reacceptance if completeness was already accepted; otherwise include the corrected state in the pending completeness review. Apply the correction flow above to blocking findings, including newly demonstrated material process violations during final review waiting, checkpointing or closeout: require explicit independent closure of such findings in Final Completeness (and renewed substantive acceptance first if substantive state changed). Do not treat verification by the Executor at Close as independent closure. Missing substantive work requires a correction batch; purely historical disposition or lifecycle record correction does not. Normal accurate recording and verification of subsequent review/closure events does not itself require another review; a previously closed historical residual alone does not trigger repetition. No additional gate.

Verify both final independent decisions and underlying provenance refer to the same effective definition, governing source versions and relevant implementation/evidence state. Require all:
- Every mandatory Brief Objective and Plan obligation addressed by verified evidence, honest limitations and valid negative/inconclusive outcomes.
- Required checks, execution status and findings truthful; no unresolved blocking finding, mandatory obligation or required human/external action.
- Both final acceptances valid for same final content.
- Task Ledger, results, review records and durable final checkpoint saved and verified; capture the completion transition time contemporaneously and persist/read back the completion decision/status and actual completion event only afterward. If completion bookkeeping is interrupted or defective, reconcile it under Recovery; do not declare verified completion with a known unresolved blocking deviation. Preserve genuinely completed history; do not require a reviewer to certify future completion events. Recover missing completion record or checkpoint by reconciliation, not by automatically repeating valid final reviews.

No default additional human closure gate. Report results, evidence/checkpoints, actual reviews, limitations, unresolved decisions if any. Report the final checkpoint and exact local/remote visibility separately; later completion bookkeeping follows the non-recursive checkpoint rule in Evidence and checkpoints. A blocker means not Completed. Preserve Completed Task history; later improvement starts a new Task.
