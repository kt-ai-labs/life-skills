# Human decisions and authorization

Executor or reviewer may flag a reserved decision with evidence, options and consequences. Executor asks the authorized human and stops dependent work. Verify authority by project rules. Record material dialogue per [Task artifacts](task-artifacts.md); authoritative decisions have actor, scope, UTC (or known provenance/unknown), exact governing version and explicit disposition. Persist/read back before relying on them.

## Resume after a human response

An Executor's request for clarification, confirmation or approval creates an input dependency, not a human-directed Task pause. On the answering turn, record material dialogue/decisions, apply the answer, verify affected prerequisites and perform the next authorized action. Continue through required reviews and work until the next required human input, other concrete blocker, explicit restriction or verified completion. Do not end the turn merely to acknowledge, save or summarize the answer, or ask for Continue. For a partial answer, apply resolved items and ask only for still-needed information; proceed with unaffected authorized work. A rejected proposal is not an available choice: revise within authority or ask about a materially different option when necessary. An explicit refusal of approval or launch leaves that gate unpassed; record it and do not repeat the unchanged request or infer assent. Respect the response's scope and restrictions: no implied separate approval, initial launch or lifting of an explicit pause, stop or withdrawal.

## Initial gates

1. One-time runtime continuation per [Start and resume](start-and-resume.md).
2. Explicit confirmation of all three reviewer roles in intended harnesses before main Discovery per [Reviewers](reviewers.md); reuse unambiguous selections already given.
3. Independent Final Task Definition acceptance after readiness.
4. Present the reviewed Brief and Plan together, highlighting fully specified proposed choices (including authority and methodology). Obtain explicit approval of each identified artifact. A clear approval can confirm a fully presented choice, not an unasked unresolved choice.
5. Obtain explicit initial launch. These are three distinct decisions, **not necessarily three messages**: an unambiguous human instruction approving both presented versions and starting Execution suffices. Persist/read back the actual combined instruction if given, then record/read back each identified approval and update its status. Mark Definition complete when both are verified, before recording/verifying launch and creating the Execution Plan. If launch has not been addressed, ask once when both are approved: Start Execution now? Record explicit refusal or pending launch in the decision record; No or absent yes leaves approved-awaiting-execution. Do not repeatedly ask. On interruption, recover each decision from its actual durable instruction and identified versions, including the combined instruction if present; finish missing bookkeeping only where this evidence is unambiguous, current and authorized. Otherwise keep the specific decision pending. Reconcile later refusals, restrictions or superseding decisions; a prior launch instruction for changed governing content is not authorization to launch the revised definition. A later explicit launch of the current approved definition suffices. Never infer approval from launch or launch from approval. No execution reviewer/work before launch.

Before launch verify current governing content and source/decision versions. If substantive content or governing inputs change, re-review affected definition/readiness and renew approvals of changed governing snapshots, even if artifact text is unchanged. Preserve an unchanged artifact's approval only if independent definition reviewer explicitly confirms compatibility; unchanged text is insufficient on its own. Record applicable versions and reasons. Metadata alone is not substantive revision. Explicit approval of a fully presented, unchanged reviewed proposal records authorization of that choice; it is not a new substantive input requiring re-review. If a response changes the reviewed content, its changed version needs review and approval; do not treat a combined instruction as approval of a later draft.

## After launch

Derived Execution Plan and routine choices have no default human gate. Honor authorized pause, stop, withdrawal and abandonment per [Recovery](state-and-recovery.md). A historic launch does not override changed permission.

For material re-entry (including confirmed profile-only change), follow [Changes and corrections](changes-and-corrections.md). Only Final Task Definition Reviewer assesses impact on both Brief and Plan and decides renewed approval for each affected artifact/version, considering last human-approved versions, cumulative amendments and governing inputs even if text unchanged; record the basis and version pointers for unaffected approvals; it cannot waive a human-reserved decision or expand actual permission. Required or uncertain → specific human approval; content changed by response → re-review. Waiver with reasons is autonomous acceptance, not human approval. Preserve effective versions and original launch.
