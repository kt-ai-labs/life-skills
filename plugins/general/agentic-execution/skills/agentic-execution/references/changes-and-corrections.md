# Replanning, re-entry and correction batches

## Ordinary operational changes

When evidence triggers a recorded material risk or contradicts an assumption, reassess affected work promptly; stop dependent work where validity or authority is uncertain. Apply an already authorized response where its conditions hold, otherwise follow the existing operational replanning or Definition re-entry boundary below. A trigger does not itself expand authority or automatically require human escalation. Record actual material impact and disposition in existing records, not a separate risk register.

Stay in Execution if Brief/Plan strategy, constraints and evidence obligations remain intact. Reorder pending Steps, update dependencies, substitute equivalent tools within the accepted strategy and constraints (never silently replace a human-required tool or workflow), add checks, add/skip a Step with justification or regroup future batches. Record reason, O-ID impact and required verification in Execution Plan/Task Ledger. Material current batch plan or prerequisite changes require renewed Planning Acceptance before affected work. No default human gate for routine decisions.

## Material definition changes

If essential methodology, required Tools & Skills, feasibility assumptions, Objectives or agreed evidence cannot be maintained, stop affected work and record re-entry into Task Definition within the same unfinished Task. Keep original launch, prior effective definition, partial work, suspended phase/batch, affected evidence and pending decisions. Record the actual re-entry/suspension and subsequent restoration or supersession events in the [Task Ledger](task-ledger.md), linking governing records and preserving original operation identities. A fallback already authorized in Plan may be within its strategy; do not invent a fallback. Even a profile-only amendment after launch requires the approval-impact gate.

Before suspending, superseding or switching executable scope, reconcile any pending job/review under [Recovery](state-and-recovery.md). Where still authorized and safe, let an existing operation settle without starting replacement work; otherwise stop or safely suspend it where authorized. Verify that it cannot continue affected execution or change dependent state before starting a replacement Current Batch or affected work under a revised definition. If uncertain, block that work, retain pending identity and preserve late results as evidence of the original scope, not acceptance under the new one. This also applies to prerequisite correction batches below.

1. Revise affected Brief/Plan and perform only needed new Single Focus Discovery; none if not needed. Follow [Task Definition](task-definition.md) and [Discovery](discovery.md): independent readiness on revised scope then fresh Final Task Definition Reviewer for this cycle. Do not execute against a pending draft.
2. That reviewer performs both substantive definition review and explicit approval-impact assessment. Compare last human-approved Brief/Plan, all subsequent effective amendments (cumulative drift), original intent, explicit constraints, reserved human decisions and optional user-defined autonomy scope. No mandatory autonomy file. Material changes in Objectives, methodology, evidence, risk or permissions are strong escalation signals. It alone can waive renewed human approval, with reasons, but not a human-reserved decision or actual authorization constraint; other actors can flag blockers. Removing, replacing, narrowing or making optional an explicitly human-required tool, Skill or workflow requires the human's explicit authorization of that change unless already covered by an explicitly authorized alternative. If required or uncertain, request authorized human approval and block dependent work pending the answer per [Human approvals](human-approvals.md#resume-after-a-human-response); revise/re-review any changes and record required approvals. If waived, record **autonomous acceptance**, never human approval.
3. Persist/read back effective definition and its independent acceptance, approval-impact decision and required human decisions. Preserve original human-approved versions separately by recoverable pointers. Reconcile already accepted and partial evidence against new obligations and sources; mark affected evidence insufficient until verified.
4. Return to Execution Planning. Update rolling horizon and reconcile every affected unfinished batch, including the full suspended dependency chain; restore or supersede explicitly (preserve partial evidence, do not claim acceptance). For each unfinished batch that will continue under the revised effective definition, obtain Planning Acceptance identifying that definition and applicable plan/prerequisite evidence when it becomes Current and before substantive work resumes, even when the amendment changes only reviewer configuration. Preserve earlier valid acceptance as historical provenance and use the compatible retained reviewer where allowed. Accepted batches remain historical; do not rerun them solely because profiles changed, and use new correction/revalidation batches as needed. If reconciliation shows no executable work remains, return through Execution Planning to Final acceptance without fabricating a batch. Verify or re-establish final decisions for the new effective definition; historical acceptance alone does not authorize closure on changed governing content. Do not repeat launch unless authorization withdrawn/changed. Fresh reviewer per new batch; retained reviewer of interrupted batch only if reliable, independent and compatible.

## Defect in accepted work

Never reopen, rewrite or retroactively replace accepted batch/checkpoint. Create a new correction batch with new B/E IDs, identify previous batch/result corrected. Analyze direct and transitive dependents; mark stale/invalid evidence promptly and block dependent execution/final closure until revalidation or independently reviewed no-impact justification. Group related corrections/checks where appropriate; no mechanical rerun of every downstream result. Normal lifecycle: fresh reviewer, planning acceptance, execution, execution acceptance, checkpoint. Strategic defect also triggers re-entry.

If prior defect appears while Current Batch is unaccepted and correction/revalidation must precede it:
- Stop dependent Steps; durably suspend original Current with ID, partial work/evidence, applicable planning acceptance, pending checks/findings and Task Ledger event. Its reviewer is idle or retired if unreliable.
- Designate prerequisite correction/revalidation as Current and complete normal fresh-reviewer lifecycle. Only one executable Current; if a correction itself needs a prerequisite correction, retain all suspended IDs and their dependency order. No false acceptance/advancement of suspended batches.
- After verified correction checkpoint reconcile downstream impact and partial evidence, restore the next eligible suspended batch or explicitly supersede it with replacement. Renew Planning Acceptance for changed effective definition or materially changed scope/prerequisites before dependent Steps resume. Preserve original IDs and historical evidence.

Completed and Abandoned Tasks are never reopened; further work is a new Task per [Recovery](state-and-recovery.md). Final-review findings in an active Task use this same correction flow, then [Final acceptance](final-acceptance.md).
