# Risk Tripwires — actionable risk definitions

The logical artifact `01-task/risk-tripwires.md` is the single living owner of material actionable risk descriptions, observable reassessment conditions and intended authorized responses. Create it when such risks exist; absence is legitimate when none are identified. Use the authorized alternative physical mapping if applicable. Do not invent risks, require risk scores/probabilities or exhaustive enumeration. Task Plan continues to own strategic methodology, assumptions, decisions and obligations; link those rather than duplicating them.

## Format and lifecycle

Use stable monotonically allocated RT001, RT002, etc.; keep IDs even when resolved/no longer applicable. Each material risk has a meaningful title, Task/Objective scope, description and potential material impact, observable trigger, intended authorized response, current applicability/status and authoritative references. Use proportionate detail. Example: RT001 — Result equivalence; O02/O03; unexplained material discrepancy on the agreed control dataset; downstream analysis may be invalid; stop dependent computation, investigate and route to authorized correction or Definition re-entry; link governing equivalence checks. This is illustrative, not a mandatory risk.

Identify/refine during Definition and Discovery; add or refine as actual Execution evidence warrants, record triggered/resolved/no longer applicable status. Preserve material prior definitions/changes via recoverable versions and actual dispositions in Task Ledger and Agentic Decisions where applicable; do not recycle IDs, silently remove/weaken material risks or conceal uncertainty. Distinguish actual material trigger/response events from planned conditions; apply [Timestamp policy](timestamp-policy.md) to events, not routine status edits.

## Authority, governing versions and changes

At Definition readiness, Final Task Definition review and existing Brief/Plan approvals, submit/present applicable recoverable risk definitions (or explicit no material actionable risks identified); identify the relevant version alongside the Plan and its governing inputs. Material risk assumptions/responses that constrain approved strategy, obligations, verification or authority participate in the Plan's governing snapshot; record exact applicable content/absence and recoverable version pointers in actual review/approval/effective-definition records. No separate approval or risk reviewer. A risk response is not authority: verify its basis in accepted strategy, permissions or explicit human decision.

Before launch, changed governing risk content requires affected Definition review and renewed approval per [Human approvals](human-approvals.md); preserve unchanged approvals only on explicit compatibility assessment. After launch distinguish routine status/clarification and operational risks within effective strategy from material governing changes. Maintain the live risk file without automatically invalidating approvals: retain the effective governing risk snapshot separately by recoverable pointer, identify changed scope/impact and applicable current risk version for affected work. New or revised operational risks within accepted strategy follow ordinary replanning; material current batch scope/prerequisite/check changes require renewed Planning Acceptance before dependent work. Any change to governing strategy, obligations, required verification or authority follows Definition re-entry, independent approval-impact assessment and required human authorization, even if Brief/Plan text is unchanged. Include old/effective/proposed risk definitions, cumulative changes and affected approvals in that review. Do not enact an unapproved pending governing change, weaken a tripwire/response to evade review, or require renewed approval solely for non-governing risk updates. Preserve reviewed versions for recovery and checkpoints; distinguish live operational version, pending proposed changes and effective governing content.

## Execution and reassessment

In rolling-wave planning identify applicable RT IDs, incorporate proportionate checks/conditions and authorized responses into relevant batches and steps; do not repeat risk content in each Objective or Step. Submit current relevant risk definitions and governing version for Planning Acceptance, verify continued applicability with actual prerequisites; no continuous polling. On trigger or contradicted assumption, reassess, stop dependent work where validity or authority uncertain, apply an already authorized response where appropriate and follow [Changes and corrections](changes-and-corrections.md). Record actual material event and disposition in [Task Ledger](task-ledger.md), and any material autonomous decision in [Agentic Decisions](agentic-decisions.md). Use RT links in plans/evidence as useful. A trigger does not automatically require human intervention. Escalate only under existing authority, strategic-change or reserved-decision rules.

## Review and recovery

Existing applicable reviews assess coverage of material actionable risks, soundness/observability of triggers, authorized responses, appropriate checks and actual dispositions, version consistency and preservation of governing obligations. Final Substantive assesses consequential risks/limitations and actual result validity; Final Completeness reconciles material risk tracking against evidence across the Task. No separate phase/gate, exhaustive risk enumeration or indiscriminate reread. Recover effective risk version, relevant live definitions/status and pending changes from authoritative version pointers and evidence; a live file is not proof of approval. Reconcile missing/partial artifacts and stop affected work if required risk content or authority cannot be established. For explicitly authorized adoption, retain earlier Plan-owned tripwires and their reviewed versions as historical authority; identify applicable ones in the new owning artifact with references, reconcile compatibility of affected gates and avoid retroactive migration of historical Tasks. No invented risks or automatic rewrite. See [Start and resume](start-and-resume.md) and [Evidence and checkpoints](evidence-and-checkpoints.md).
