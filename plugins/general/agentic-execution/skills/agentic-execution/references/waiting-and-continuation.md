# Waiting and continuation — harness-independent contract

## Pending work and waiting

Apply the [Progress invariant](../SKILL.md) and this contract before dispatch, on pending invocation and affected recovery. For Codex, read [Codex harness guidance](harness/codex.md) before the first independent review or long-running invocation that may require waiting, or on recovery if applicable guidance is not reliably current. Reuse verified capabilities while their basis remains current. The harness reference selects the mechanism; the following lifecycle rules remain authoritative.

Applies to every review and long-running job. Complete submission checks before dispatch. Before invoking a review or long job that may outlive its call/turn, persist/read back pending intent and recovery basis in the appropriate authoritative record (review round for reviews, Task Ledger for jobs); correlate the Task Ledger event by stable identity. Record the actual invocation and available handle/receipt (or its absence) as soon as returned, before waiting or yielding. Missing identity does not prove absence. Intervene before waiting only for a specific outstanding independent authorized obligation within the current Single Focus that materially advances the Task without affecting the submitted scope or bypassing a gate, or to handle a new material event, restriction, failure or recovery need. The independent-work exception does not authorize starting another Assignment, E-Step, batch or review. **Waiting discipline:** Never perform any operation merely to fill waiting time, maintain apparent activity, keep the turn active or create another opportunity to receive a completion event. This applies regardless of tool, command, read, write, verification, analysis or commentary; replacing one waiting substitute with another is not compliance. Redundant checks of unchanged files, redundant rereads of submitted material and speculative preparation do not qualify. Required submission and acceptance checks, readback, invocation recording and contemporaneous material-event timestamp capture remain obligations; do not extend them into speculative or repeated probes. If no qualifying obligation or event requires action, genuinely wait next. After recovery of a pending invocation, apply the same rule once the required reconciliation is complete. Prefer the longest suitable wait to repeated short/default polls. A process/session yield, timeout, notification or empty output is not by itself proof of current liveness, progress or completion. Never simulate waiting with text or promises. Report material verified transitions/errors or requested/required updates, with the last observed state/time clearly distinguished from current status.

Repeated near-immediate or premature returns without meaningful invocation-specific evidence indicate an ineffective wait. Reconcile once and switch to a supported effective lower-overhead mechanism if available; if returns still prevent genuine waiting, report the wait blocker rather than spin. A genuinely elapsed capped short blocking wait is not itself a blocker: use the longest suitable interval while justified, without extra status queries after each ordinary timeout. Reassess at a proportionate elapsed-time/no-progress threshold using actual liveness, failure or result evidence and expected duration; do not reset the threshold on empty, duplicate or unrelated events. Healthy silent computation may justify extending the threshold; mere liveness is not progress. If no effective safe wait is available or state cannot be established at reassessment, report the concrete blocker and preserve pending state for Resume; do not stop after an arbitrary number of returns. Never cancel, relaunch, accept or advance merely because a wait timed out.

Honor new human restrictions immediately; use responsive waits as required. Before consuming results or advancing, reconcile invocation/result identity and affected governing state, not the entire Skill on each wakeup. When no qualifying obligation or event requires other action, choose the next action directly: (1) consume a verified available result; otherwise (2) yield to verified automatic continuation if applicable, including reliable terminal/failure delivery, interruption and any necessary reassessment deadline; otherwise (3) use effective native blocking waiting, or (4) another verified safe and supported wait if native waiting is unavailable or ineffective. If none is effective, apply the blocker/reassessment rules above. No separate human confirmation for an already-authorized fallback. End the turn with pending work only for an authorized human pause/termination, verified automatic continuation without a new human turn with recoverable pending state, or a concrete blocker under the waiting/reassessment rules above. A completion notification alone does not establish automatic continuation. Do not terminate an authorized turn merely because a result is pending or another tool call might be inconvenient.

Continue through authorized stages until a real blocker, human pause/termination, or verified completion. Report meaningful verified progress, not stale status; never promise unsupported background work. At completion give results, evidence, limitations and both final decisions. A pending gate is not success.
