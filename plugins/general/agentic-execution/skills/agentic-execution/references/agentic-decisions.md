# Agentic Decisions — material autonomous decision history

The root task artifact `agentic-decisions.md` is a concise human-facing history, not a second governing Plan, authority record, review record or event Ledger. Initialize it for new Tasks before D00, after authorized storage and governing identity are established; a heading with no entries is valid. Use the authorized alternative physical mapping if applicable. Its coverage spans Definition, Discovery, Execution Planning, Execution, replanning/corrections, Definition re-entry, recovery and final acceptance/closeout.

## Materiality and recording

A decision is material when knowing it could reasonably change the human's assessment of the result, agreement with strategy, understanding of consequential trade-offs, confidence in evidence or willingness to accept risks. Examples: consequential methodology/assumption, excluding material evidence, substantial reuse instead of recomputation, changing verification coverage or criteria, accepting material limitations, or choosing a consequential response to a risk. Equivalent refactoring, naming, interchangeable utilities, reorganizing tests without changing assertions or coverage and routine implementation choices normally do not qualify. Removing meaningful coverage or weakening a check does. Examples are not exhaustive; do not manufacture decisions or record routine activity.

Capture the actual decision promptly when made, not only at approval or closeout. A proposed choice awaiting review/authority is not a decision already exercised; record material proposals explicitly as proposed, and append the actual adoption, withdrawal or supersession when it occurs. Record operational consequences in their owning authoritative artifact; this history summarizes and links them. A reviewer recommendation is not an Executor decision; preserve the actual independent response in the review record and capture the resulting material Executor choice if applicable. Record material events in Task Ledger where required, not every AD entry as a redundant event. Reuse timestamps for the same actual event per [Timestamp policy](timestamp-policy.md).

## Format and history

Use stable monotonically allocated AD001, AD002, etc. Each entry has meaningful title, actual decision time and provenance, relevant phase/scope, what and why, material alternatives/consequences/trade-offs/limitations as applicable, and pointers to authoritative artifacts/evidence and related AD/RT/LE IDs where useful. Distinguish proposed, exercised, superseded, withdrawn and human override/revocation dispositions in plain language; no mandatory state machine or extensive template. Append only. Never reuse IDs, rewrite/delete prior entries or fabricate decisions/times. Correct mistakes and record changed decisions by new entries referring to the originals. Before appending, reconcile the last allocated identity and relevant history from the actual file; verify the append and preservation of existing content proportionately; do not repeatedly reread unchanged history. If competing writes, uncertain IDs or partial records prevent safe continuation, reconcile under Recovery before affected writes. No deterministic helper required.

## Human override and revocation

A human can question, override or revoke a decision within actual authority. Honor restrictions immediately. Preserve the authoritative human instruction and disposition in `01-task/decisions.md` and material dialogue in request.md under [Human approvals](human-approvals.md). Append a new AD entry referencing the original, stating overridden/revoked by human, actual disposition, authority reference and affected work/evidence; this is a disposition of an agent decision, not a competing human authority record. Do not delete the original when asked to abandon its approach. Reassess dependent work and already accepted evidence; use [Changes and corrections](changes-and-corrections.md) and required approvals, including new correction batches if needed. No retroactive permission. Agent reconsideration or supersession likewise preserves original history. Effective decisions remain in their owning records, not inferred from AD history alone. A human override does not itself grant authority beyond its actual scope or pass a review gate.

## Review and recovery

At existing Human Review Focus boundaries present the identifiable full governing documents and navigate relevant AD entries; no separate approval. Existing Discovery/Definition, Planning/Execution and final reviews assess applicable material decision coverage, provenance, authority consistency and operational consequences using actual evidence and the relevant submitted version/prefix, not exhaustive session replay. Final Completeness checks whole-task coverage proportionately. Final Substantive examines decision consequences relevant to correctness. Subsequent accurate dispositions/closeout entries are reconciled under existing review/close rules; changed substantive claims or blocking omissions require affected review, not recursive recording of review activity. Do not fabricate future entries.

On authorized adoption by an unfinished Task, preserve prior governing records; initialize only the newly applicable history with a truthful adoption boundary and links to recoverable earlier decisions as useful. Do not retroactively require historical AD entries or assign unsupported dates/decisions. If recovering a current missing or interrupted history, distinguish absent pre-adoption history from loss of required records; restore from recoverable versions and actual evidence or disclose gaps, block affected work when required provenance/authority cannot be established. Use relevant entries and authoritative artifacts to recover, not blanket rereads. Do not alter closed Tasks or silently migrate a governing version. See [Start and resume](start-and-resume.md), [Recovery](state-and-recovery.md), [Evidence and checkpoints](evidence-and-checkpoints.md).
